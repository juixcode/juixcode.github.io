Introduction

Who I am

My story N°01
Juix
Engineering Student Minecraft Creator Web Developer

I'm Julien, but I go by Juix online. My handle and logo nod to a few things: my first name (the straw forms a 'J'), fruit juice (my childhood nickname), and tech (UI, UX). I'm an engineering student at ESILV in Paris.

These days, I love building Minecraft datapacks and websites. It always starts with a real need or a fun idea: I sketch it out, then decide if it's worth a deep dive. If it is, I build it carefully brick by brick, using AI to level up my coding or learn new stacks. If not, I vibecode it: I leverage AI from start to finish to ship the idea fast, stepping in as the tester and QA lead.

Passions

What I love

Ever since I was a kid, I've loved building and creating: it started with Legos and cardboard crafts, then moved to Minecraft blocks. I also spend tons of time on sandbox and management games—anything where I'm free to design, build, and optimize. Diving into web development was just the natural next step.

Side projects

Minecraft Packs

I build and publish Minecraft datapacks that bring fun items and fresh mechanics into the game. It's essentially built-in modding, powered by Minecraft's own native scripting system.

Modding

Party Hub

A collection of icebreaker mini-games built brick by brick for hangs with friends and family. Done with overpriced party apps! The goal is to bring all trivia and prompt games together for drinks or Discord hangouts, all in a modern, smooth, and 100% free app.

Learning Tools

I use AI to vibecode lightweight tools that help people learn faster and smarter by embedding AI features directly into the apps. In engineering school, learning efficiently is key if I want to keep time for side projects. I share them freely with my classmates and friends.
